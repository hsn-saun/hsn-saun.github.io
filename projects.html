Projects

Videos from experiments and projects.

  1. Autonomous Hexapod Robot using Deep Reinforcement Learning

    Undergraduate thesis, Hanoi University of Industry · 2023 · graded 94, selected as best thesis

    Deep reinforcement learning for the hexapod’s locomotion and outdoor autonomy, from training in simulation to deployment on the hardware, compared against a differential-drive mobile base.

    YouTube

  2. 18-DOF Hexapod Robot for Autonomous Operation Outdoors

    Hanoi Intelligent Robotics Laboratory, Hanoi University of Industry · 2023

    An 18-degree-of-freedom hexapod, with three joints in each of its six legs, built to walk and explore on its own across uneven outdoor terrain.

    YouTube

  3. Autonomous Mobile Robot with Deep Reinforcement Learning for Real-Time Obstacle Avoidance

    Hanoi Intelligent Robotics Laboratory, Hanoi University of Industry · 2023

    A mobile robot drives to its goal through an unknown environment, avoiding obstacles in real time with a policy learned by deep reinforcement learning. The controller was developed in Gazebo and then validated on the physical robot.
